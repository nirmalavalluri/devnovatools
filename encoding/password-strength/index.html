❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What makes a password strong? ▾
A strong password is at least 12 characters long and uses a mix of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers and special characters. Avoid dictionary words, names and predictable patterns.
How long should my password be? ▾
At least 12 characters for regular accounts, 16+ for critical accounts like email and banking. Length is the single most important factor — a random 20-character password beats a complex 8-character one.
Should I use a password manager? ▾
Yes — password managers generate and store strong unique passwords for every site. You only need to remember one master password. Popular options include Bitwarden (free), 1Password and Dashlane.
