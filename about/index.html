👋 My Story

I'm an indie developer focused on building practical tools that solve real problems for developers, designers and everyday users. I believe the best tools are fast, distraction-free, and accessible to everyone — regardless of budget or location.

Over the years I've noticed that developers constantly switch between dozens of small utilities — a JSON formatter here, a JWT decoder there, a regex tester somewhere else. My goal with DevNova Tools is to bring everything together in one place, so you can stay focused on what matters: building great software.

DevNova Tools is my largest and most ambitious project — a unified platform offering 107+ free developer tools across JSON, APIs, DevOps, frontend, security, text processing, encoding, database tools and more.

I built this platform because developers deserve tools that just work — no popups, no login walls, no distractions. If these tools help you build faster or solve a problem, that's the best reward.
— Nirmala Valluri, Creator of DevNova Tools
