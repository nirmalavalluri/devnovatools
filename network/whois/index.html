📖 What is this tool?

WHOIS Lookup retrieves domain registration information including creation date, update date, country and registrar details. Use it to check if a domain is available, find the owner, or verify registration details.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is WHOIS? ▾
WHOIS is a protocol for querying databases that store information about registered internet resources including domain names. It tells you who registered a domain, when, and through which registrar.
Why is some WHOIS data hidden? ▾
GDPR and privacy protection services allow domain owners to hide personal contact information. Many registrars offer privacy protection that replaces personal data with proxy information.
How do I find the owner of a domain? ▾
Enter the domain name and click Lookup. If privacy protection is not enabled, the registrant's name and contact details will appear. Otherwise, you'll see the registrar's proxy information.
