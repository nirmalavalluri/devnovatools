Lorem Ipsum Generator creates placeholder text for design mockups, prototypes and layout testing. Generate any number of paragraphs, sentences or words of the classic Lorem Ipsum text. 📝 Why Lorem Ipsum?

Lorem Ipsum has been the industry standard placeholder text since the 1500s. It has a natural distribution of letters and word lengths that closely resembles real text, making it better for layout testing than repeated "test text" or random characters. The text is based on a section of Cicero's "De Finibus" (45 BC).

⚠️ Common Mistakes

  • Shipping with placeholder text — always search for "Lorem ipsum" before deploying to production
  • Using for content length estimation — Lorem Ipsum words average 5-6 characters. Your actual content language may differ significantly.
  • Using in user testing — real users get confused by Latin text. Use realistic content for usability testing.
